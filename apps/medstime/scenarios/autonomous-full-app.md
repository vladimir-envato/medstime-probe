## Goal

You just installed this app. Go through onboarding and allow everything it
asks for: notifications and alarms, on the app screens and on the system
alerts. Buy the subscription on the first paywall. Then explore the whole app
in detail and try every option you can find: every tab, row, toggle, picker,
sheet, and form, including Add Medication with different forms, units,
frequencies, and times, editing, archiving, and deleting medications, the
schedule on other days, PDF export, and the app's own Settings tab. Change
values and change them back, and look for anything broken or inconsistent.

Never tap these, anywhere in the app:
- Manage Alarms, Manage Notifications, Open Settings, or Open iPhone Settings,
- Cancel Subscription, Manage Subscriptions, or Restore Purchases,
- Rate us, Contact us, or Share,
- Print on the PDF Preview screen (Save is allowed).

If you open Privacy Policy or Terms of Service, close it right away with the
X button. If another app comes to the front, go back with the "◀ MedsTime"
link in the top-left corner.

There is no fixed end; keep trying new options until the step budget runs out.

## Persona

Curious user who pokes at every option, not tech-savvy.
