## Goal

You just installed this app. Get through onboarding, buy the subscription on
the first paywall, then spend most of the run in Add Medication and the screens
around it. Try a different flow each time, for example:
- different forms and units (tablet, capsule, mL, drops, puff, injection),
- frequencies: daily, every few days, specific days of the week, cyclic
  (take days and break days),
- one dose a day and several times a day; change times and dose amounts,
- going back from a later step and changing an earlier choice,
- empty or very long names, zero or odd doses, then cancel halfway,
- editing a medication you added, and deleting one,
- adding a medication on another day in the schedule.
There is no fixed end; keep trying new flows until the step budget runs out.

## Persona

First-time user, not tech-savvy, never seen this app.
