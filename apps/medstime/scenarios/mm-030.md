## Goal

MM-030 removed old, unused medication categories and split the forms of each
category into general forms and more specific forms. Check that every category,
form, icon, and text is correct, that the Schedule group summary names each
medication by its general form, and that nothing shows a raw key such as
`medication-type-...`, `medication-specific-type-...`, `medication-dose-form-...`,
or `medication-time-row...`, a blank label, a placeholder icon, or a wrong
singular or plural.

### 1. Onboarding and purchase

You just installed the app. Go through onboarding:
- Allow notifications and allow alarms right away, both on the app's own screens
  and on the system alerts.
- On the first paywall, choose the **Monthly** plan (1 month, "Mesečno" in
  Serbian) and buy it. Confirm the purchase sheet.

### 2. Categories and forms

Open Add Medication, type any random medication name, and go to the
medication-type screen.
- Read every category under Common Types (Tablet, Capsule, Oral liquid, Drops,
  Injection) and More Types (Inhaler, Spray, Patch, Ampoule, Infusion, Oral
  preparation, Rectal preparation, Vaginal preparation, Cream / Gel / Ointment,
  Powder / Granules, Solution / Suspension, Skin / Hygiene, Suppository /
  Insert, Implant / System, Other). In Serbian the three route categories read
  "Oralni preparat", "Rektalni preparat", and "Vaginalni preparat". There must be
  exactly these 20, each with an icon and a translated title, and none of these
  old names as categories of their own: Syrup, Eye dropper, Nasal spray,
  Ointment, Pestle, Vitamine, Mouthwash, Suppository, Solution, Suspension,
  Other type.
- Open every category that has forms and read the whole screen, scrolling to the
  end. It has two sections:
  - The first section lists the general forms. Its header reads "Generic Form"
    ("Opšti oblik") with one row and "Generic Forms" ("Opšti oblici") with more.
    Categories named by one form (Tablet, Drops, Injection, Oral preparation, and
    the like) show one row: the category itself. These categories list their
    general forms instead:
    - Cream / Gel / Ointment: Cream, Gel, Ointment, Paste, Oil, Lotion, Foam
      (Krema, Gel, Mast, Pasta, Ulje, Losion, Pena)
    - Powder / Granules: Powder, Granules
    - Solution / Suspension: Solution, Suspension
    - Suppository / Insert: Suppository, Insert
    - Implant / System: Implant, System
    - Skin / Hygiene: Skin Care Product, Hygiene Product (Preparat za negu kože,
      Higijenski preparat)
  - The second section, "More Specific Medication Form" ("Precizniji oblik
    leka"), lists the more specific forms.
- On every form screen, check for duplicates: every label must appear only once
  across both sections, including the general row, so no more specific form may
  repeat a general form or the category name. Two known cases must stay fixed:
  Inhaler lists Metered Dose Inhaler only once, and Injection does not list a
  more specific form named just "Injection". Check every other category the same
  way and report every duplicate you find.
- Every label must be translated, and each specific form must fit its category,
  for example Nasal Spray under Spray, Mouthwash under Oral preparation, Eye
  Drops under Drops, Ophthalmic Ointment under Cream / Gel / Ointment,
  Toothpaste under Skin / Hygiene.
- Go back without choosing, except where step 3 needs a choice.

### 3. Add eight medications

Use random names, except for Fluorinse. On the dose step and the summary, check
the dose unit with one unit and with more than one. After choosing a form, the
category row on the medication-type screen shows the chosen more specific form
under its title. With a general form or the category's own row, it shows no
second line that repeats the category name.

08:00 daily:
1. Tablet › Oral Tablet, 2 tablets (also try 1 and 1.5 before setting 2).
2. Tablet › the general row Tablet, 1 tablet.

09:00 daily:
3. Cream / Gel / Ointment › Topical Lotion (more specific form), 1 application.
4. Cream / Gel / Ointment › Lotion (general form), 1 application.

10:00 daily:
5. Catalog medication: type `Fluorinse` on the name step and pick the
   suggestion. This brand name is the one exception to the rule of entering only
   made-up names: it is needed to check a medication from the app's catalog, and
   no personal data is involved. It must land in Oral preparation › Mouthwash with
   the **mouthwash** dose unit. Set 2.
6. Drops › Eye Drops, 1 drop, then 3 drops.

11:00 daily:
7. Injection › the general row Injection, 1 injection. Reopen the form screen
   and check that the Injection row is checked.
8. Skin / Hygiene › Toothpaste, with the dose unit the app suggests.

If the step budget is getting low, skip the form lists you already read rather
than a medication.

### 4. Cross-checks

- Schedule tab, today. Each group summary names every medication by its general
  form, never by its more specific form, with the singular for one medication of
  that form and the plural for two or more. Expect, in any order:
  - 08:00: "Tablets" ("Tablete")
  - 09:00: "Lotions" ("Losioni"), because Topical Lotion and Lotion are both
    lotions
  - 10:00: drops and oral preparation ("kapi" and "oralni preparat"), not
    mouthwash
  - 11:00: injection and hygiene product ("injekcija" and "higijenski preparat"),
    not toothpaste
  Expand each group and read each row's dose ("2 tablets", "1 tablet", "3 drops",
  and the other doses in the app's wording).
- Medications tab: each medication shows its category icon and title, and its
  details show the right category, form, and dose unit. Details may show the more
  specific form, for example Topical Lotion.
