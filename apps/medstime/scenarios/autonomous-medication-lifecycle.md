## Goal

Get through onboarding (buy the subscription on the paywall), then manage
medications through their whole life cycle, over and over:
- add several medications with different forms, frequencies, and times,
- delete some of them and check they are gone from the list and the schedule,
- archive some, check where archived medications show up, then unarchive them
  and check they are back in the list and the schedule,
- mix the order: archive then delete, unarchive then edit, add one with the
  same name as an archived one.
After each change, check the Medications list and the Schedule agree.

## Persona

Organized user who keeps the medication list tidy, not tech-savvy.
