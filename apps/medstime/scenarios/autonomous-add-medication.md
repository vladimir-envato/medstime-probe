## Goal

Get through onboarding (buy the subscription on the paywall), then open Add
Medication and stay inside its screens for the whole run. Never submit: do not
tap the final Done or Save that creates the medication, and do not leave the
flow through Close or Cancel. Move back and forth through the steps and try to
break things, for example:
- different forms and units (tablet, capsule, mL, drops, puff, injection),
- frequencies: daily, every few days, specific days of the week, cyclic
  (take days and break days), and switching between them,
- one dose a day and several times a day; add, change, and remove times,
- go back from a later step, change an earlier choice, go forward again and
  check the later steps still match,
- empty, very long, or emoji names; zero, huge, or decimal doses,
- tap Next fast twice, rotate through pickers, dismiss the keyboard mid-edit.
If you leave the flow by accident, open Add Medication again right away.

## Persona

Curious user who pokes at every option, not tech-savvy.
