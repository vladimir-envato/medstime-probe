## Goal

Onboarding is skipped: the app opens on the Schedule tab. If a paywall
appears, buy the subscription. Then move fast and a bit wild: tap whatever
catches your eye, switch tabs, open and close sheets, start flows and abandon
them halfway, go back, type short nonsense into fields, swipe lists, and tap
the same control twice in a row. Do not stop to plan; pick the next action
from what is on screen right now. Report anything that crashes, freezes,
shows wrong data, or leaves the app in a strange state.

## Persona

Impatient user who taps quickly and never reads, not tech-savvy.
